Dunkin Donuts

About Dunkin Donuts

Dunkin Donuts is my favorite place to get fast comfort food. I love the variety of donuts they have and the coffee is always fresh. The staff is soo friendly and the service is pretty quick. I highly recommend trying their new menu! My favorite thing to order recently has to be the loaded hash browns, a few pumpkin spice munchinks (like 10), and a medium pink pineapple refresher with sparkling water. It is soooo good!

Omars truck

About Omars truck

Omars truck is a food truck next to my house that serves some of the best street food in the city. They have some of the best halal food ever! I love getting my lamb over rice there for only $8! The staff is friendly and I highly recommend checking out Omars truck if you're looking for great food.

The District

About The District

The District is a restaurant right next to my house. It has a great atmosphere and the food is always delicious. I love getting their burgers with french fries. They are always so crispy and delicious!
